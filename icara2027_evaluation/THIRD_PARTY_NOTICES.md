The RGMP research builds upon the original PlaceIt
Quality-Diversity robotic placement framework.

Original authors:
- Amina Ferrad
- Johann Huber

Institution:
Institut des Systèmes Intelligents et de Robotique
(ISIR), Sorbonne Université, France.

The original PlaceIt copyright and permission notice
are retained in the repository LICENSE file.

RGMP extends the placement evaluation framework with
geometric prioritization and experimental validation.

## YCB Object Models

The reproducibility materials contain model directories

- Banana
- Bowl
- Mug
- Plate
- Power Drill
- Scissors
- Spoon

YCB Object and Model Set:
https://www.ycbbenchmarks.com/

Reference:
Calli et al., "YCB Object and Model Set:
Towards Common Benchmarks for Manipulation Research,"
2015.

The original provenance and licensing of the exact
mesh copies included in this archive must be verified
before public redistribution.

## Reproducibility Scope

The RGMP evaluation includes archived placements,
geometric ranking scripts, independent Panda evaluation
records, TIAGo experimental analyses, ablations,
sensitivity studies, and supporting numerical results.

The archived numerical analyses have been verified.
The complete grasp simulation and physical experiments
have not been independently reproduced from a clean
installation.
